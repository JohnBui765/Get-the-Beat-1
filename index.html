      The cleanest sound path

      Echo Stems mixes at 44.1 kHz, the rate the engine works at, in 32-bit floating point, with no limiter. If your output device runs at another rate, Chrome converts it with a high-quality converter. To avoid any conversion, open Windows Settings > System > Sound, choose your output device, and set Format to 24 bit, 44100 Hz. Switch Audio enhancements off there too, so Windows doesn’t colour the sound.
